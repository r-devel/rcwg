### Status of S7

- Was not included R 4.6.0 - may be targeting 5.0.0 release, not sure, but still a way off.

- Currently a reasonable number, but not very large number of packages depending on S7 (<https://cran.r-project.org/package=S7>)

  - ggplot2 switched over from S3 to S7 last year in major release (4.0.0 <https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/ggplot2/news/news.html>), which was a big step forward

- Still quite a bit to do:

  - Some changes made in base R to allow arbitrary new object-oriented system to be added in.

  - There are still some outstanding issues, e.g. working on compatibility with S4.

- So experimental at the moment, but worth exploring!

- Ella Kaye giving a talk about writing a package that uses S7 at useR! 2026 - she will probably share her slides afterwards, maybe at the office hour.
