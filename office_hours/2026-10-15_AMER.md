## Discussion

- Making a proposal for "data.frame.unordered" class
    - As just an idea at this stage, process is to float idea on R-devel mailing list to see if there is positive feedback
        - Why should this be in base R vs a package?
        - What is the motivation/use case?
            - No need to load into memory
            - May not know number of rows in advance, e.g. streaming data
        - What impact would it have in terms of extent of change to R code base or on R packages.
    - Could start in a package and later port into R, but a consensus on the approach would be useful.
    - Timing: R Core will be working towards 4.6.2 release on 29 Oct, then will be travelling for Rousseeuw Prize Ceremony in first week of November, so may be better after that
    - Maybe prepare proposal for discussion at a future office hour, could advertise in advance
 - R Core stance on AI
    - Generally positive as long as contributor reviews and can explain/defend each line of code
    - AI bug reports can be unnecessarily verbose - plan to add guidance about reporters reviewing any AI-generated reports
    - AI might be good at making/reviewing patches that involve changing many lines of code - but do we trust the changes?
        - Possibly for systematic changes could at least do sanity checks, e.g. patch only modifies calls to `stop()`, coupled with spot checks
