#### Ways to contribute

* Help with translation for localization of message strings: https://translate.rx.studio
* Reporting a bug: https://bugs.r-project.org/
* Testing R before a release: https://blog.r-project.org/2021/04/28/r-can-use-your-help-testing-r-before-release/index.html
    * Needs doing just before a release: typically around March each year
* R Dev Days for working with others on contribution: https://contributor.r-project.org/events/r-dev-days/

     * Expect to have one in Montpellier next summer.

* Can also contribute to translation of recommended packages on weblate: https://translate.rx.studio/projects/the-r-project-recommended-packages/
